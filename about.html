Kritima Arora

I’m a design manager who likes the whole stack: strategy, systems, people, and now AI.

At Delhivery, I lead a team of 6 owning the Operational UX charter, from First Mile, Mid Mile and Last Mile to Returns and the Human Resource Management System (HRMS). Right now we’re building AI into how we design, to cut the time from design to pilot.

Before this, I led design systems for 8+ products at Upstox and designed storefronts and payments for Kindle India at Amazon.

I’ve also taught and worked with students at design schools including ISDI Mumbai and NIFT Chennai, founded a tweed label, taani studio, and host a podcast, Creative Climb.
