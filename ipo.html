Redesigning IPO for improved activation and retention

  • Scaling a feature from 1-10
  • Activation

Team and Role

Duration

3 months

Current status

Live on Upstox app

My Role

Led the project end-to-end by defining product strategy and UX flows, deriving research insights, crafting functional UI & prototypes, testing, and aligning all stakeholders.

Overview

With more companies going public and strong short-term gain potential, investor interest in IPOs is rising. This requires us to improve the overall user experience of IPO, as it benefits us not only by generating revenue but also by fostering long-term customer retention. Moreover, the current IPO platform is only meeting the bare minimum requirements and often leads to user dissatisfaction, as reflected in the high volume of support tickets.

So let’s try to redesign the IPO experience!

Objective

Improve discovery
Facilitate informed investment decisions
Maintain transparency throughout the process
Retaining current users
Adopting new ones

Understanding the domain

What is an IPO?

An IPO is when a company sells shares to the public for the first time, allowing people to become shareholders and helping the company raise funds for growth

How does an Investor benefit from investing in IPOs?

Investing in IPO has the potential for high returns as they allow early access to the companies and give impressive short-term profits through listing gains. In 2023, 51 out of 60 IPOs (85%) saw a listing gain, 30 saw gains over 20%, only 9 (15%) had negative returns.

  • Analyse the IPO better

Qualitative research findings

  • Users want to easily discover IPOs and stay updated throughout the process until it closes
  • The IPO timeline is highly appreciated and stands out as an appreciated feature of Upstox
  • The top three factors considered by those analyzing an IPO are company fundamentals, valuation, and the business model.
  • Bidding dates, minimum investment, GMP, and subscription data are considered highly useful information.
  • Users often turn to platforms like Chittorgarh, Google Search, Tickertape, and Moneycontrol to review IPO details before submitting their applications

Information overload

The overall look and feel of both the approaches were perceived as quite overwhelming and cluttered, lacking the prioritisation of the data points for shortlisting. This proved the hypothesis of offering more data points upfront to be incorrect.

Step back and rethink

  • Min investmentNot required upfront

    Almost all the mainboard IPOs have min investment in the range of 13k-14k, so not a differentiator

  • Price rangeNot required upfront

    Price range is used to analyse the growth potential of a company

  • Closing dateRequired

    Creates urgency and helps users plan their investment

  • Subscription dataRequired

    Subscription data offers social validation and is a key factor in IPO selection

  • Status, action, industry, company name and logoRequired

    Basic details and action required to make a decision

The most crucial step - Approve mandate

🤔

Why is it so important?

The application is considered as complete and submitted to exchange only when the mandate is approved by the user, failing to do so will lead to application rejection

🧐

Why do users fail to approve?

Post application users receive a mandate approval request on their UPI app with a delay of 1 to 24 hours. This time delay often leads them to forget to approve, resulting in application rejection

💡

How can we solve this?

Remind users to accept mandate when it is ready, on regular intervals, at multiple visible touch points across the app

Communicate the delay well and in advance to set the expectations upfront

Modify application

Problem

In order to edit the application details users are canceling their existing applications and submitting new ones. Due to delay in API responses, multiple applications are being submitted to exchange, which increases the risk of rejection.

Around 3-4% of applications placed are cancelled

Solution

Allow users to modify/edit the existing application. This will help us in lowering down the number of application failures, TCDs raised and cancellation requests

The 30-second version

I redesigned IPO on Upstox around discovery, informed decisions and status transparency — to cut support tickets and grow participation.

The problem
The IPO flow met only the bare minimum. In hot markets it drove ~4,500 support tickets a month (peaking at 3×, with ~90% abandon rates), and only 5–6% of active users applied.
What I did
Led it end-to-end over 3 months. Research traced tickets to discoverability, status updates (75% of complaints) and missing information. I designed one scalable IPO card for every stage, status-based categories, an Analyse screen for both quick-gain seekers and long-term holders, a near one-click application, mandate-approval reminders with a personal timeline, and modify-application to stop cancel-and-reapply.
The goal & status
Targets: ~80% fewer support tickets, 3× IPO revenue and 10% active-user participation per IPO. The redesign is live on the Upstox app.
